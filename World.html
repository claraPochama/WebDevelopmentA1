    In the Forgotten Realm...

    Phandalin, a wonders built on the Forge of Spells, a beacon of civilization on the Sword Coast North... But it's been 300 years ago.

    The frontier and its wild outskirts hold more than simple roads and weathered stone. In these quiet corners and shadowed paths, truths lie buried beneath rumor and dust. It is here, among these scattered landmarks, that their schemes take root… and the tale of Phandalin begins to unravel.


    Stonehill Tavern

    Every adventure starts in a tavern. This warming, peaceful shelter located in the center of the town, providing delicacies and tales from all over the continent. If you take a sit next to the fireplace and hold your breath, maybe you might overhear the secrets untold that lurk under this tranquility...

    Shrine of Luck

    Here rests the Shrine of Luck, dedicated to the Lady of Good Fortune, Tymora. Once tended by the elven priestess Sister Garaele, the altar now lies in uneasy silence. The townsfolk whisper that she vanished without a trace. What secret drove her to disappear? And who silenced her before it was spoken?

    Miner's Exchange

    The Miner's Exchange serves as Phandalin’s unofficial center of commerce, a place where independent prospectors bring ore, rumors, and troubles in equal measure. But in the quiet corners of the Exchange, deals are struck that never touch the scales. And its master, Halia Thornton, is a merchant who gathers more than just precious metal.

    Cragmaw Castle

    The ruin rises among the forested hills, its shattered walls barely holding the weight of old conquest. Though abandoned by its builders long ago, the fortress now becomes a name whispered among unwelcomed visitors in the town. Whatever secrets the castle guards, they are kept with claws, arrows… and silence.
