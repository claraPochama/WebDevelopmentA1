    "There is no hero in this realm."

    Five adventurers, five schemes.

    The town Phandalin welcomes them — saviors come to bring light to the old mine, yet beneath every smile lies ambition, and behind every blade, betrayal.


    A bard forever hiding his face behind a silken veil, weaving lies sweeter than songs.

    A cleric of a goddess of fortune, preaching blessings cruel as fortune.

    A tiefling reaper, harvesting lives with a scythe and a wide smile.

    A young drow wizard, cold as the caverns that birthed him.

    And an elven ranger — pure in heart, violent in deed.


    They come not to save Phandalin, but to claim it.
