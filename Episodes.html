    Season 1: The Lost Mine of Phandelver

    Led by Dungeon Master Luny
    Ep. 1: What Is in A Name

    An usual errand sends an elf, a drow, a tiefling and a robed stranger to an unusual town. Before they find out each other's name, on the dusty way to Phandalin, they disocver a hidden cave crawling with goblins and unanswered questions.

    Ep. 2: Good Fortune

    The town welcomes the gang with rumors and uneasy glances. Meantime, a newly arrived cleric begins her own search for a missing sister.

    Ep. 3: The Dirty Old Town

    A tense encounter with the Redbrands ends up with open hostility between the gang and Phandalin’s feared ruffians. Determined to end the threat — or at least claim its spoils — the party sets their sights on the gang’s hidden stronghold.

    Ep. 4: The Tresendar Manor

    What awaits beneath Tresendar Manor is more than thugs and shadows… and far more dangerous to provoke.

    Ep. 5: The Banshee

    The party is hailed as unexpected heroes of Phandalin. Their newfound acclaim sends them beyond the town’s borders, seeking the elusive banshee Agatha for answers about Sister Garaele’s disappearance.
